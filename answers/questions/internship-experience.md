On the backend, I opened the Python FastAPI and MongoDB service with its first commit and grew it to 89 REST endpoints and 7 Kubernetes CronJobs on AWS EKS, with about 1,450 offline tests gating every merge in CI. I stood up the production deployment on day 3 with Helm, Jenkins, and Docker, set up Prometheus and Grafana, and fixed a five-day silent CronJob outage.

On iOS, I shipped the app to the App Store (about 6,000 installs and 1,119 peak daily users) with Firebase auth, Sign in with Apple, server-driven UI, and Live Activities. I also built a personalized LLM news alert pipeline and helped other teams debug their services, including a 65% failure rate in a shared LLM pipeline.

Beyond code, I worked with legal during App Store review, set up the team's Jira backlog, and onboarded the team's second engineer.
