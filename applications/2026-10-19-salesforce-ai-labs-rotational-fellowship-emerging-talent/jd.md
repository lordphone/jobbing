## The Experience

Build at enterprise scale. Launch like a founder.

Salesforce AI Labs is seeking exceptional emerging AI and computer science talent for a highly selective, 24-month rotational fellowship designed to develop the next generation of AI builders.

Across four six-month rotations, Fellows tackle real technical and customer challenges, build with frontier AI technologies, and ship solutions into production at enterprise scale, alongside researchers, engineers, and technical leaders, with AI-native development tools and large-scale compute.

Rotations:
- Build (Product Engineering): design, build, and ship AI-powered products; production-scale engineering.
- Shape (Product Management): translate customer needs into product strategy, define priorities.
- Deliver (FDE / Builders): work directly with customers to develop and deploy AI solutions.
- Transform (DET / Customer Zero): reimagine enterprise workflows and operations with AI.

After the fellowship, Fellows transition into AI teams across Salesforce.

## What You'll Actually Be Doing

- Build and Ship AI Solutions: design, prototype, and deploy AI-powered applications, agents, and enterprise solutions.
- Work Across AI Labs: AI agents, process generation, conversational AI.
- Partner with Enterprise Customers: with field, product, and engineering teams, build, customize, and deploy AI solutions for customer use cases.
- Own End-to-End Development: concept through experimentation, development, and production deployment.
- Shape the Future of AI Products: translate customer insights and technical discoveries into product improvements.
