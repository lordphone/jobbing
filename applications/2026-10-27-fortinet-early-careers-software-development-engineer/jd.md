## Job Description

As a Software Engineer, you will:

- Work with developers within the team and on other cross-functional projects, as well as project management, to develop and maintain the product
- Develop and maintain software components on current and future products
- Be hands-on in bug fixing current products
- Implement software features of high quality and on schedule
- Integrate, maintain, and improve both 3rd-party and proprietary code
- Contribute to the design and implementation of software systems, frameworks, and new features
- Communicate effectively, both verbal and written

## We Are Looking For

- 0-2 years of experience in software development, including internships, co-ops, academic projects, or relevant coursework
- B.S. in Computer Science or Electrical Engineering; M.S. is a plus
- Experience with C/C++ and Unix/Linux through coursework, projects, internships, or professional experience
- Familiarity with REST API semantics and their application in networking appliances
- Familiarity with libcurl for formulating REST API calls is a plus
- Familiarity with JSON data objects and their creation and parsing
- Familiarity with multi-process/thread programming using POSIX and system programming concepts
- Understanding of networking fundamentals, specifically TCP/IP networking, switching, and routing
- Understanding of principles of network management
- Knowledge of general QA procedures and methodologies as well as software development fundamentals
- Experience with embedded systems is a plus
- Experience with networking principles is a plus
- Experience with firewall configurations and operation is a plus

Must be authorized to work in the U.S. without sponsorship.
