# Software Engineer, Early Career — Notion

San Francisco, California · Full time · Department: Early Career · Onsite · $130,000 – $150,000 (SF/NYC)

> This is NOT a new grad role! This role is for candidates with 0-2 YOE and can start full time right away.

## About the Role

Help shape core user experiences and accelerate how people discover value in Notion. Own projects, make technical decisions, work across design, product, and data. Hiring across several teams; the recruiter matches you to a team.

## What You'll Achieve

- Plan, build, and ship product features from conception to launch, then iterate based on insights and customer feedback.
- Improve performance, reliability, and quality of key experiences used by millions of users and thousands of organizations.
- Run experiments that drive activation, retention, collaboration, and revenue, partnering with design, data science, and research.
- Build internal tools and platform improvements that help all engineers ship quickly and safely.
- Contribute to team norms, code quality, and a culture of learning and thoughtful tradeoffs.

## Areas you might work on

- Core Notion product features: editor, databases, sharing, other key areas.
- AI and automation: intelligent features that make Notion more powerful, contextual, and efficient.
- Growth and activation: help new users discover value, improve onboarding, increase engagement.
- Platform and infrastructure: backend services, reliability, scalability, performance.

## Skills You'll Need to Bring

- Less than two years of engineering experience. Solid fundamentals in data structures, algorithms, and distributed systems; customer-minded, pragmatic.
- Thoughtful problem-solving: understand context, navigate ambiguity, decompose complex problems, balance business impact.
- Impact-driven approach to technology: tech as a tool for user impact; stays current with Cursor, Claude Code, and other AI-assisted development tools.
- Proactive communication and high agency: own your work, communicate progress and blockers, drive projects forward.

## Nice to Haves

- Experience with any part of the stack: React, TypeScript, Node.js, Postgres, Elasticsearch.
- Cares about the interaction between technology and society.
- Knows computing pioneers like Ada Lovelace, Douglas Engelbart, Alan Kay.

Every Notino is expected to be curious and use AI as a real collaborator; roles where AI fluency is core say so explicitly.
