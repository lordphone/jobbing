# Chicago Trading Company (CTC) — Associate Engineer - 2027 Start

Chicago, IL; New York, NY (same job; pick one and set location preference in the application)

Chicago Trading Company (CTC) is a premier proprietary trading firm specializing in options market making. Our collaborative culture fuels innovation in quantitative research, systematic trading strategies, and cutting-edge trading technology. For over three decades CTC has provided critical liquidity across derivatives exchanges worldwide - making them fairer, more transparent, and more efficient.

Are you excited to play an important role in building next generation trading infrastructure and applications? Do you thrive in an innovative environment? If so, we encourage you to consider CTC!

## The Role

We are looking for talented and motivated software engineers to join our engineering teams in designing, coding, and testing sophisticated trading tools and risk applications. These distributed real-time systems will require you to have a deep understanding of computer science concepts, strong software development skills and methodology, and effective communication and collaboration abilities. Software development cycles at CTC are short and releases of proprietary software are frequent. In this fast-paced and dynamic team environment, our engineers quickly see the results of their effort, and the impact it has on our businesses. Engineers will also have exposure to trading through our combined trading and technology curriculum which includes a shadow on one of our trading desks.

## What it takes for you to be successful

- You can apply understanding of data structures, algorithms, and multi-threading concepts to real-world problems
- You are curious about options trading, pricing and risk management. No prior knowledge or experience in finance is needed
- You are a highly motivated, humble, and collaborative team member
- Ability to thrive in a team-based environment filled with driven people who want to compete and win together
- Ability to effectively communicate ideas both written and verbally that showcase your analytical skills

## What We're Looking For

- You are pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a similar technical field with Java, C++, or Python experience
- Strong foundation in an object oriented programming language gained through coursework or internships
- You will graduate between December 2026-June 2027
- You have a demonstrated record of success (academics, awards, etc.)
- Willing to relocate to Chicago, IL or New York, NY (most positions will be based in Chicago with a limited few in New York)

## Why CTC

- Fast-growing, industry-leading global trading company
- Environment that actively explores and applies emerging technologies — including AI — to enhance trading, engineering, and research workflows
- Collaborative team environment; excellent growth potential
- Free breakfast and lunch, fitness center membership

## Notes

- One application per position per cycle (e.g. Associate Engineer and Quant Trading Associate). Chicago and New York posts are the same job — do not apply to both.
- Salary: $200,000 USD, plus annual discretionary bonus.
- Applications may be reviewed by automated systems, including AI.
