## Overview

The Windows Ecosystem and Commercial Engagement (WECE) team brings Microsoft's Windows vision to life through a global network of partners, customers, and engineering teams. We turn strategic priorities into innovative experiences across Windows devices. Within WECE, the Partner Engineering Platform (PEP) team builds secure, reliable, and compliant services, data pipelines, and agentic solutions that enable collaboration across these groups.

We are seeking a Software Engineer to design, build, test, and operate cloud services that power collaboration between Microsoft and its partners. You will work on real-world engineering problems across APIs, distributed systems, security, data processing, and service reliability while learning from experienced engineers.

You will grow quickly by working on production systems used across Microsoft and its partners, learning how to build services that remain correct and available as usage, data volume, and integration complexity increase. You will ask good questions, raise risks early, and take pride in delivering secure, observable, and maintainable code. Your work will help Microsoft and external partners securely collaborate, exchange information, and manage critical engineering workflows at scale.

Success in this role requires a solid engineering foundation, curiosity, a growth mindset, and the ability to take a defined problem and drive it to completion with increasing independence.

## Responsibilities

- Design, develop, test, deploy, and operate secure and scalable cloud services and APIs with guidance on larger design decisions.
- Own features end to end, from technical design and implementation through deployment and production support.
- Build reliable solutions involving APIs, data processing, authentication and authorization, and asynchronous workflows.
- Write high quality, maintainable, well-tested code and contribute to unit, integration, and automated test coverage.
- Implement authentication, authorization, data synchronization, and asynchronous processing logic that correctly handles retries, failures, and edge cases.
- Add telemetry, logging, and alerts to the features you build, and use them to monitor service health and diagnose issues.
- Participate in on-call rotation, live-site support and Investigate production issues and contribute to service reliability and operational improvements.
- Participate in design reviews and code reviews, incorporating feedback, and contributing to engineering quality.
- Collaborate with engineers, program managers, and partner teams to clarify requirements and deliver working solutions.
- Learn and apply team engineering standards, security and compliance practices, and responsible use of approved AI-assisted development tools.
