# Whatnot — Software Engineer, 2027 New Grad

- Location: New York, NY; Los Angeles, CA; San Francisco, CA; Seattle, WA (hybrid; must live within commuting distance of a hub)
- Employment type: Full time
- Department: Early Career / Engineering New Grads
- Compensation: $155K – $160K base + equity

## About

Whatnot is the largest live shopping platform in North America and Europe to buy, sell, and discover the things you love — trading cards, fashion, electronics, live plants, and more. Remote co-located team with hubs across the US, UK, Ireland, Poland, Germany, and Australia. Named #1 Best Startup Employer in America by Forbes.

## Role

Looking for intellectually curious, highly motivated individuals to join post grad in 2027. The most important skills are making sound product decisions and shipping high-quality products fast. Engineers with great product instincts who can own whole projects from start to finish. High autonomy; good trade-offs and judgment are essential.

Duties include:
- Building new features for users in our live-streams
- Implementing growth tactics and strategies to scale our buyer and seller flows
- Building systems at scale that help us deliver a high trust marketplace experience
- Writing product or system development code
- Designing, implementing, and operating our most critical and scalable services
- Carefully evaluating the technical tradeoffs of every decision
- Supporting features at every phase, from design through implementation and launch
- Triage product or system issues and debug/track/resolve by analyzing the sources of issues, network, or service operations and quality

## You

Comfortable figuring things out as you go, biased toward action, curious about what you're building; care about outcomes more than credit; stay close to the product and its users.

Should have some industry software engineering experience, plus:
- Graduated with a CS or Software Engineering degree by Summer 2027
- Problem solver and out-of-the-box thinker
- Willing to take risks and build product and systems for 1M+ users
- Capable of building scalable systems
- Excellent product instincts — think about users first rather than the best technical solution
- Known for shipping products and features lightning-fast
- Ability to pick up new technologies very quickly
- Open to relocation to Los Angeles, New York City, San Francisco, or Seattle

## Benefits (summary)

Time off, health insurance, WFH allowances, wellness/childcare/family-planning allowances, 401k with 4% match, monthly dogfooding allowance (all employees use Whatnot as buyer and seller), 16 weeks parental leave.
