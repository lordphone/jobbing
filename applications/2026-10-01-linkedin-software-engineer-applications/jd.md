# LinkedIn — Software Engineer - Applications

- Location: Mountain View, CA (hybrid)
- Employment type: Full-time
- Career Track & Grade: IC2/7
- Department: Engineering
- Pay range: $108,900 – $147,400 (plus possible bonus, stock, benefits)

## Responsibilities

- Scale distributed applications, make architectural trade-offs applying synchronous and asynchronous design patterns, write code, and deliver with speed and quality.
- Develop multi-tier scalable, high-volume, reliable user-centric applications that operate 24x7.
- Produce high-quality software that is unit tested, code reviewed, and checked in regularly for continuous integration.
- Provide technical leadership, driving best engineering practices to initiate, plan, and execute large-scale, cross-functional, company-wide critical programs; identify and evangelize opportunities to improve engineering productivity.
- Own end-to-end feature delivery, translating ambiguous product requirements into clean, testable, maintainable production code.
- Write rigorously unit-tested, peer-reviewed software deployed using robust CI/CD pipelines.
- Utilize AI tools throughout the SDLC to boost design, coding, and review productivity.
- Design extensible APIs and backend services that handle synchronous and asynchronous workflows, making deliberate trade-offs between speed and scalability.
- Design and optimize data layers, selecting relational or non-relational database architectures to handle distributed transactions and large-scale data storage.
- Demonstrate technical ownership: code health, thorough documentation, and comprehensive post-mortems.
- Operate with agility, delivering high-impact features with speed and operational quality without compromising stability.
- Work with Product Management, Design, and Data Science to build intuitive, reliable, user-centric application experiences.
- Learn to navigate and contribute to highly complex, large-scale enterprise codebases and infrastructure.
- Learn to leverage advanced telemetry, monitoring, and profiling tools to proactively diagnose and resolve production bottlenecks.

## Basic Qualifications

- BA/BS in Computer Science or related technical field or equivalent practical experience
- 1+ years of industry experience in software design, development, and algorithm-related solutions
- Experience programming in languages such as Python, Java, JavaScript, C/C++, C#, Objective-C, Ruby, etc.

- 2+ years of relevant work experience.

  - Proficiency in at least one mainstream language (Java, C++, Python, or JavaScript/TypeScript).

  - Modern development ecosystems: advanced IDEs, build automation, containerization, automated testing frameworks.
- Proven track record of transforming ambiguous business opportunities into autonomous agentic systems capable of long-horizon reasoning and decision-making.
