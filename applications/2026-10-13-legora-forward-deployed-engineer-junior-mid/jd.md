- Department: Engineering (Enterprise Engineering)

## About Us
Legora is redefining how legal work gets done. Not built for lawyers, built with them. AI-native workspace for legal professionals: analysing thousands of documents in minutes and powering end-to-end workflows. 2,100+ customers across 80+ countries (Cleary Gottlieb, Goodwin, Linklaters, White & Case, Dentons, Barclays). $200M+ ARR; teams across Europe, North America and APAC; acquisitions including Qura, Walter AI, Graceview, Cadastral, and Wexler.

Values: We lean in (ownership over titles, outcomes over intentions). We fight for excellence (high standards, direct, ego-free feedback). We grow together.

## The Role
As a Junior/Mid-level Forward Deployed Engineer, you'll work directly with enterprise customers to understand their needs and build the software that connects Legora to their systems and workflows.

You'll build integrations and contribute to Legora's core product, taking defined projects from technical scoping through implementation, testing, and production delivery. You'll learn to turn customer needs into practical engineering solutions, with support from experienced engineers on more complex architecture and delivery decisions.

Hands-on IC role within Enterprise Engineering, working closely with product engineers, Legal Engineers, and Engagement Managers.

## What You'll Be Doing
- Work with enterprise customers and your team to understand workflows, clarify requirements, and translate customer needs into engineering tasks.
- Build, test, and ship integrations and product changes, taking ownership of defined projects from implementation through deployment.
- Explain technical decisions clearly, ask useful questions, and keep customers and teammates informed about progress and blockers.
- Work with experienced engineers on design reviews and technical tradeoffs, seeking support early when requirements or risks are unclear.
- Debug issues across Legora and customer systems, using logs, tests, and systematic investigation to find and resolve problems.
- Bring customer feedback into product discussions and identify opportunities to make solutions and tooling reusable.

## Who You Are
- 2-3+ years of software engineering experience, with hands-on experience building and shipping production software.
- Strong software engineering fundamentals. You can write, test, and debug reliable code and work effectively in an existing codebase.
- A clear communicator who is comfortable talking with customers, asking questions, and explaining technical work to engineers and nontechnical stakeholders.
- Experience taking a feature or defined project through implementation and delivery. You can explain what you built, the decisions you made, and the outcome.
- Curious about the problem behind a request.
- Accountable for your work, receptive to feedback, and able to surface blockers early.
- Adaptable across technologies and interested in learning unfamiliar systems quickly.

## Nice to Have

- Experience working directly with customers or partnering with product teams to understand user needs.
- Familiarity with APIs, authentication, integrations, and data flows across systems.
- TypeScript experience. We use it extensively, but welcome strong engineers from other stacks.
