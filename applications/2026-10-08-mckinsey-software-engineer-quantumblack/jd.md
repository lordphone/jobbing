You will work as a core member of a multidisciplinary engineering team alongside senior engineers, designers, and technologists — to build and deliver high-quality software that creates real impact for clients across industries.

You will contribute across the full stack, implementing features and fixes while pairing closely with teammates to sharpen your craft and adopt engineering best practices. You'll collaborate with both technical and non-technical colleagues, developing a firsthand understanding of how great software teams operate.

Your work will help solve some of the most complex and high-impact challenges facing clients across industries. Collaborating across QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey and QuantumBlack Labs teams, you'll help develop innovative software capabilities and scalable enterprise solutions. You'll help build robust technical foundations for production-ready, AI-enabled systems. Your contributions will directly support the firm's ability to accelerate AI adoption, solve complex business problems at scale, and enable clients to achieve meaningful, lasting impact.

You'll be based in one of our North America offices as part of our global Software Engineering community. Working in cross-functional teams, you'll collaborate closely with designers, product managers, and industry experts to deliver software solutions. By partnering with clients from technical teams to C-level executives, you'll help solve complex problems that drive tangible business value and begin to build your skills as a client-facing technologist.

Your work offers a unique opportunity to grow at the forefront of AI and software engineering. You'll develop expertise at the intersection of technology and business by tackling diverse challenges in the evolving AI landscape. Working alongside multidisciplinary teams, you'll gain a holistic understanding of how software engineering enables advanced AI while collaborating with some of the leading technologists and AI practitioners in the industry.
