# Manulife — Associate Applied AI Engineer – GenAI Systems

- Location: Toronto, Ontario (Hybrid)
- Full time · Posted 2026-10-05 · End date: October 19, 2026
- Job requisition id: JR26091743
- Salary: $69,525 – $115,875 CAD + incentive

Manulife is making a significant investment in Advanced Analytics and GenAI to transform how Finance, Treasury, and Actuarial teams make decisions. Our AI team builds practical, governed solutions that move from idea to implementation and are adopted in real business workflows.

We're hiring two Associate Applied AI Engineers who bring strong technical depth, sound software engineering practices, and a modern GenAI design mindset to help deliver AI and GenAI capabilities that integrate effectively into real business workflows. Excellent opportunity for candidates with graduate-level training in AI/ML or related quantitative disciplines, and those in early-career applied AI roles, who want to work on meaningful business problems rather than research prototypes or notebook-based analysis alone.

## Responsibilities

1. **End-to-end solution design (GenAI + ML)** — translate business problems into a solution approach (user workflow, data flow, model approach, evaluation plan, operational controls); lightweight design artifacts (system context, runtime sequence, agent/tool maps, data lineage, decision logs); trade-offs across accuracy, explainability, cost, latency, maintainability.
2. **Build models and GenAI components for Finance & Actuarial** — forecasting, classification, NLP, anomaly detection, optimization, scenario analysis; RAG, structured summarization and extraction, transaction understanding, variance explanation, tool-using workflows; feature engineering from structured and unstructured data.
3. **Evaluation and testing** — holdouts, backtesting, error analysis, fit-for-purpose metrics; GenAI evaluation via scenario coverage, edge cases, human review rubrics, quality scoring, regression testing; document limitations; guardrails.
4. **Productionize and operate** — with Data Eng, ML Eng, Software teams: data pipelines, model packaging, CI/CD, deployment, monitoring; maintainable tested code (version control, modular design, logging, code review); monitor data quality, drift, performance, operational failures; runbooks, adoption, UAT.
5. **Governed environment** — model risk review documentation (assumptions, validation results, monitoring plans, UAT evidence); privacy and security (data minimization, access controls); reproducibility, traceability, auditability.
6. **Grow team capability** — learn from reviews; reusable components, templates, testing patterns; bring forward emerging GenAI engineering patterns.

## Required qualifications (preferred section repeats these verbatim)

- Master's or PhD in CS, Statistics, ML, Applied Math, OR, Engineering, or related quantitative field.
- 0–3 years applied DS/ML, including internships, co-ops, research; strong academic projects considered.
- Strong Python and SQL; Git workflows, code reviews, unit and integration testing, logging, readable structure, debugging, basic performance tuning.
- Hands-on DS/ML tooling: scikit-learn, PyTorch/TensorFlow, Spark/Databricks or similar; data prep, feature engineering, model development.
- Turn a problem into a structured technical approach: inputs, outputs, assumptions, failure modes, evaluation.
- Exposure to building or evaluating GenAI: at least one of RAG, structured summarization/extraction, LLM-based classification, tool/function calling, multi-step workflows.
- Strong evaluation mindset across ML and GenAI: metric selection, holdout testing, error analysis, scenario coverage, edge cases, basic regression testing.
- Production-oriented development: packaging, APIs/services, configuration, monitoring outputs, maintainability.
- Strong communication: explain technical outputs, limitations, and design choices in plain language.
