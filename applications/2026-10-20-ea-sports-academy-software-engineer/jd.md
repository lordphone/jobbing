- Role ID: 216357

- Studio: EA Studios - SPORTS
- Worker type: Regular Employee

## Description

Join the 2027 EA SPORTS SE Academy, a full-time program in Orlando, Florida, designed to accelerate growth as a Software Engineer. Permanent role at Electronic Arts; first year focused on guided learning, building relationships, and technical development.

Year 1:
- Three-week immersive onboarding: first two weeks in person at the Vancouver campus (travel covered), third week at the local studio (Orlando or Vancouver). Hands-on technical training, studio culture, cohort, teammates, mentors.
- Then join an EA SPORTS development team as a permanent full-time Software Engineer, with monthly cohort-based SE Academy learning sessions across Systems, Gameplay, Animation, UI, Rendering, and more.

After Year 1: continue in the permanent role on the EA SPORTS team, connected through the Academy alumni network.

Must be available for a May 2027 start, in Orlando for onsite orientation at Tiburon (downtown Orlando), then travel to EA Vancouver, return to work onsite at Tiburon, then transition to a full-time hybrid position.

## Responsibilities

- Participate in monthly learning modules throughout the first year as part of the Academy cohort.
- Embedded in an EA SPORTS development team:
  - Collaborate with designers, content creators, and software engineers to deliver features for a cross-platform game.
  - Write clear and estimable technical design specifications.
  - Use C/C++ to ship tested and high-quality technical implementations that meet design requirements.
  - Follow best practices to maximize game performance across multiple devices and platforms.

## Qualifications

- B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Computer Science, or equivalent training and professional experience.
- Proficiency in C++ programming.
- Demonstrated knowledge of good software engineering practices.
- Strength working in a collaborative team environment.
- Experience solving challenging engineering problems with creative technical solutions.
- Willingness to participate in professional development activities.
- 1+ years of experience with another programming language (C# or Java) and/or platforms (mobile, game consoles) is a major plus.
