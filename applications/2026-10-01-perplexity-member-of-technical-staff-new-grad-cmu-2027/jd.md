# Perplexity — Member of Technical Staff (New Grad) - CMU 2026-2027

- Location: San Francisco
- Employment type: Full time
- Department: University Recruiting

This is the application portal for CMU students and recent graduates applying for full-time Member of Technical Staff roles at Perplexity. New grad cohorts get mentorship, top-of-market compensation, and work on hard problems in frontier intelligence.

## [September 2026] On-campus interview results

Perplexity received hundreds of strong applications for CMU on-campus interviews. It ran 35 on-campus interviews the week of September 14 and picked 6 CMU students for full-time MTS offers. On-campus invites have stopped, but exceptional CMU students can still apply through this form for ad-hoc consideration.

## School-specific qualification criteria

Strong CMU candidates typically have all of the following:

1. GPA of ≥3.88, averaged across all Math, CS, and ECE courses.
2. Grade of A in 15-451, 15-651, 15-750, or 15-850.

Additional advanced coursework (systems, AI/ML, advanced math, other rigorous subjects) is highly welcome.

## Non-qualifying applications — evidence of exceptional ability

Applicants who do not meet the criteria may still apply. Perplexity will look for other evidence of exceptional ability, and applicants should briefly describe it in the "Anything Else" question at the bottom of the form.

## CMU coding contest

A strong result on the Perplexity Coding Contest counts as strong evidence of exceptional ability. The CMU contest was September 14, 2026 (5:00–7:30pm, TEP 3801). Prizes: Perplexity Max, API Platform credits, and final-round interviews for top contestants.

## Early career talent at Perplexity

- Breadth: new grads work across the stack, from Perplexity's agent harnesses to its AI inference and training systems. Many key features and systems were shipped by people fresh out of school.
- Depth: new grads are expected to go deep and often become world experts in a niche problem space.
- Mentorship & growth: new grad cohorts are well below 10% of the technical team; each person has many mentors.
- Recognition: early career pay is set well above top tech companies and startups; growth in role is rewarded.
- Mission: "AI for people who expect more" — frontier intelligence for discerning users.

## Fellowship note

The portal is for full-time roles only, not internships. Short-term work goes through the separate Perplexity Research Fellowship (https://pplx.ai/fellowship).
