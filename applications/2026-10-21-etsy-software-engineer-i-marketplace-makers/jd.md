## What's the Role?

Etsy is looking for a Software Engineer I to join the Marketplace Makers team. Marketplace Makers are the sellers who create the best buyer experiences on Etsy, and our team builds the programs and tools that recognize and grow them. We work across experiences such as the Star Seller program as well as seller tooling in Shop Manager.

As a Software Engineer I, you will build and ship full-stack web features in PHP and React/TypeScript, learn how Etsy's platform works end to end, and grow your craft with support from a team that pairs, reviews, and teaches. This is an early-career role: we are looking for someone with a solid programming foundation who is eager to learn how production software is built at scale.

This is a full time role reporting to the Engineering Manager of Marketplace Makers.

## What's this team like at Etsy?

- We are a multi-functional product team distributed across the US and Mexico. The team is composed of software engineers, a product manager, a product designer, a researcher, an analyst, and a copywriter.
- We work on problems that directly affect millions of sellers: how they earn and keep Star Seller status, and how we motivate the practices, like fast responses and on-time shipping, that create great buyer experiences.
- We ship in small increments behind feature flags, run A/B experiments to validate changes, and use data to decide what to build next.
- At Etsy, we believe that code is craft and that small, empowered, self-motivated teams can do big things. We take advantage of our continuous deployment system and cultivate a blameless culture based on trust and a commitment to learning. Learn more on our engineering blog: Code as Craft (https://www.etsy.com/codeascraft).

## What does the day-to-day look like?

- Build and maintain well-scoped product features across our PHP backend and React/TypeScript frontend, with guidance from senior engineers.
- Translate designs and product requirements into production-ready code, and write tests that keep it working.
- Participate in code review, both giving and receiving feedback.
- Help instrument and analyze A/B experiments on seller-facing features such as the Star Seller dashboard and seller inbox.
- Investigate bugs and support issues, and communicate clearly about what you find and how you fixed it.
- Collaborate with product, design, research, and analytics partners in planning, standups, and retrospectives.
- Of course, this is just a sample of the kinds of work this role will require! You should assume that your role will encompass other tasks, too, and that your job duties and responsibilities may change from time to time at Etsy's discretion, or otherwise applicable with local law.

## Qualities that will help you thrive in this role are:

- A foundation in software engineering, from a degree in computer science or a related field, a bootcamp, or equivalent self-directed experience.
- Some experience building web applications, through internships, co-ops, personal projects, or professional work, in languages such as PHP, Python, Ruby, Java, Go, or JavaScript/TypeScript.
- Curiosity about how things work and the persistence to debug until you understand the root cause.
- Comfort asking questions, taking feedback, and learning in the open.
- An interest in solving user problems and in writing code that is readable and maintainable.
- Clear written and spoken communication with both technical and non-technical teammates.
