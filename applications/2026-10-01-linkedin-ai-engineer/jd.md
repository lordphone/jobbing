# LinkedIn — AI Engineer

- Location: header says Mountain View, CA; body says Sunnyvale, San Francisco, or New York City (hybrid)
- Employment type: Full-time
- Career Track & Grade: IC2/7
- Department: Engineering
- Pay range: $120,000 – $195,000 (plus possible bonus, stock, benefits)

## Responsibilities

AI is at the core of how LinkedIn connects more than a billion members to opportunity across jobs, sales, marketing, content, and trust platforms. As an AI Software Engineer you will own end-to-end machine learning systems that run in production at LinkedIn scale. You won't just train models; you will own the recommender and classification systems that power LinkedIn's core products. Responsibilities cover the full product lifecycle: translating product requirements into system design, training the models that power the system, driving the experiments that prove efficacy, and managing the GPU fleets that run them at scale in milliseconds.

This is an individual-contributor role: own a well-defined significant workstream, align technical direction within your team, review code and technical designs, and deliver measurable member and business impact with a sense of urgency.

## What success looks like in your first year

- Grow to own an AI workstream end to end and ship at least one model-based system to a measured impact on member or business value.
- Become the go-to point of contact for your domain and help others onboard to it.
- Implement an efficiency or quality improvement (inference/training efficiency, engineer velocity, or tech-debt removal) backed by data.
- Operate systems reliably as on-call and work with senior engineers to root-cause at least one significant production regression.

## Why LinkedIn

AI systems with measurable impact on more than a billion members; recommendation systems at scale powered by open-source generative models and GPU inference; encouraged to publish at conferences, journals, and blogs. Principles: Impact, Leadership (influence, not authority), Execution (urgency), Craft.

## Basic Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related technical field or equivalent technical experience
- 1+ years experience with machine learning, data mining, and information retrieval or natural language processing
- 1+ year of industry experience in software design, development, and algorithm-related solutions
- 1+ years experience in programming languages such as Java, Python, etc.

## Preferred Qualifications

- 2+ years of relevant AI/Machine Learning experience
- MS in Computer Science or related technical discipline
- Experience with PyTorch or similar deep learning frameworks
- Experience with Spark for data manipulation and transformation
- Experience with A/B testing at scale in a consumer-facing product
- Experience with AI code development (e.g., Claude Code, Codex, Copilot)
- Experience adapting pre-trained LLMs to production systems, including fine-tuning and student-teacher model paradigms
- Published work in academic conferences or industry circles
