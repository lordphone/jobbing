## Job Summary
Sitting inside the Product and Engineering team, the Client-Data team bridges data requirements between our front-end applications and engineering services. Our work spans software engineering, machine learning, and data science at scale — from platform health monitoring and performance tracking for new releases to synchronizing instrumentation, metrics, and data-related decisions across platforms. Day to day, our work directly impacts the business through application engagement monitoring, anomaly detection, and more.

The team leverages AI-powered tools and generative AI to accelerate development, automate data quality checks, and surface actionable insights from telemetry data at scale. This includes integrating AI/ML products — intelligent anomaly detection, AI-assisted code generation, and natural language interfaces for data exploration — into both internal tooling and consumer-facing experiences. Team members are expected to stay current with the evolving AI landscape and identify opportunities to apply foundation models, RAG, and other AI-driven approaches to improve observability, streamline instrumentation pipelines, and drive smarter, data-informed decision-making.
