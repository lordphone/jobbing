# Chewy — Software Engineer I (R31182)

- Locations: Plantation, FL · Boston, MA

## Our Opportunity
Chewy is seeking a motivated Software Engineer I to join our Supply Chain IT Domain Services team in Plantation, FL or Boston, MA.

This team owns a suite of critical services that support inventory visibility, purchase order and transfer workflows, electronic data exchange, vendor compliance, and operational analytics across Chewy's supply chain network. These systems span distributed services and data pipelines supporting both real-time and batch processing.

As an early career engineer, you will work with experienced engineers to design, build, test, deploy, and support reliable software. You will take ownership of well-scoped work while developing strong engineering, operational, and business fundamentals.

This is not a purely backend role. You'll contribute across backend services, APIs, data workflows, and user-facing applications when needed, while learning to deliver complete solutions in a complex, system-heavy environment.

## What You'll Do
- Develop and maintain software using Java, Spring Boot, AWS, and related technologies with guidance from experienced engineers
- Deliver results on well-scoped features and technical improvements from implementation through testing, deployment, and production validation
- Build and support APIs, event-driven integrations, data workflows, and, when needed, user-facing applications
- Write clear, maintainable code and participate in code reviews, design discussions, and team engineering standards
- Create automated unit and integration tests and help improve the team's testing and delivery practices
- Operate in the details by using logs, metrics, traces, dashboards, and runbooks to troubleshoot issues and improve reliability and performance
- Participate in production support and on-call responsibilities with documented procedures, mentoring, and team support
- Use approved AI-assisted development tools responsibly to accelerate learning, coding, testing, documentation, and troubleshooting while validating generated output
- Put customers first by partnering with product, program, business, and engineering teams to understand needs and deliver practical supply chain solutions; earn trust by communicating progress, risks, and blockers early
- Apply spec-driven development (SDD) practices, with guidance, to translate requirements and acceptance criteria into reviewed designs, implementation plans, discrete tasks, tests, and delivered code
- Act as an owner for assigned work and contribute to operational excellence by improving documentation, observability, support readiness, and production stability

- Understanding of data structures, algorithms, object-oriented design, debugging, and software testing fundamentals
- Familiarity with relational databases, SQL, REST APIs, source control, and modern software development practices
- Exposure to cloud-based application development and an interest in building on AWS

- Ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced environment while balancing delivery, learning, and quality
