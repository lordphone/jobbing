## Company Description

Finstock is an investment and trading support company that provides personalized advisory services, in-depth financial analysis, and customized strategies for a wide range of clients. The organization focuses on the U.S. stock market while also supporting forex and cryptocurrency trading, offering diverse investment opportunities for individual and institutional investors. By combining fundamental, technical, and quantitative analysis, Finstock delivers data-driven insights that help identify promising investments and align strategies with clients’ financial goals. The company integrates advanced AI technologies to process large volumes of financial data, improve risk assessment, and provide predictive analytics and personalized recommendations. Continuous research and adoption of new financial technologies enable Finstock to stay ahead of market trends and deliver innovative, high-performing solutions.

## Role Description

This is a full-time remote role for a Software Engineer. The Software Engineer will design, develop, and maintain backend services and APIs that support Finstock’s AI-driven investment tools and analytics platforms. Daily responsibilities include implementing scalable, secure, and reliable software solutions, writing clean and efficient code, and performing code reviews and testing to ensure quality and performance. The role involves collaborating with data scientists, quantitative analysts, and product teams to integrate models and business requirements into production systems, as well as troubleshooting issues and optimizing existing services. The Software Engineer will also contribute to technical documentation, participate in agile development processes, and help improve development practices and tooling.
