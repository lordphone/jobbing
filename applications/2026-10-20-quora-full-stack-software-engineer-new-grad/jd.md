# Full Stack Software Engineer, New Grad — Quora (Remote)

- Location: Remote — US or Canada
- Employment type: Full time
- Department: Engineering (Quora Core Product team)

Quora is a privately held, remote-first company. This position can be performed remotely from anywhere in the US or Canada (see careers.quora.com/eligible-countries).

## About Quora
Mission: grow the world's collective intelligence. Two platforms: Quora (global knowledge-sharing platform, millions of monthly unique visitors) and Poe (cloud workspace where millions of users run multiple AI agents on shared context and tools). This role works on the Quora product.

## About the Team and Role
The Quora Core Product team builds the most critical features of the Quora product and owns the continuous evolution of the user experience, at the intersection of software development, design, and data science.

As a Software Engineer on the Quora Core Product team, you will:
- Have significant impact on features essential to the Quora community: user engagement, user growth, generating informative and engaging content using AI, high-quality content through effective moderation, and the creator experience
- Leverage data and experimentation to create solutions that reach millions of users
- Deliver engaging features in a fast-paced environment at high standards of quality and performance

## Responsibilities
- Develop full stack applications with Python and React
- Contribute to projects that shape the core product experience — growth, healthy moderation, empowering creators
- Design and launch product features at massive scale, using data and experimentation to guide each iteration
- Drive projects from ideation to production using the full technology stack
- Collaborate with designers, PMs, engineers, and data scientists to prototype, implement, and analyze impact
- Participate in the team's on-call rotation

## Minimum Requirements
- Availability during coordination hours (Mon–Fri 9am–3pm Pacific)
- 2025 or 2026 graduate with or pursuing a B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in CS, Engineering, or related field
- Foundational understanding of data structures, algorithms, and software design
- Experience with Python or similar, or ability to learn quickly
- Passion for learning and improving yourself and the team

## Preferred Requirements
- Previous SWE experience via internship, work experience, and/or coding competition
- Familiarity with Python, React, TypeScript and/or SQL
- Passion for Quora's mission
- Curiosity about AI and hands-on experience with AI tools or AI-powered projects
