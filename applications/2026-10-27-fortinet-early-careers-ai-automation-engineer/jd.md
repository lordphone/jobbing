## About the Role

We are looking for a new graduate to join our automation engineering function. The team builds and operates internal systems that turn large volumes of operational data into reliable, auditable workflows - and applies LLM-based evaluation only where output quality can be measured. You will work across the team's current systems and future automation projects, applying the same design playbook - validation, idempotency, evaluation, human review - to every workflow you touch. This is not just integration work: you will design the pipelines, define what "correct" and "well-scored" mean, measure output quality against ground truth, and catch the ways these systems get things wrong. This role comes with high ownership: we define the contract and test plan before implementing, keep deterministic logic where possible, and document what we build. You do not need to know every item in our stack on day one; you do need to learn quickly and ask good questions. This is a role we expect to grow - the shape of that growth is still being defined.

## Key Responsibilities

Systems & Pipelines
- Own the team's services end to end: deployment, monitoring, troubleshooting, and runbooks
- Build and harden ingestion pipelines: API integrations, retries, idempotency, validation, and handling of late or incomplete data

AI Workflows & Evaluation
- Design prompts and pipelines for summarization, classification, retrieval, and scoring
- Calibrate against human-graded samples; measure accuracy, consistency, and bias
- Ship AI features only when they meet the quality bar, with human review for high-stakes or low-confidence decisions

Content & Data Quality
- Maintain structured content, metadata, and validation checks
- Review content for leakage, staleness, and drift, and track pipeline health over time

Workflow Automation
- Automate end-to-end processes from intake through reporting
- Turn manual, repetitive workflows into reliable tools and help move repositories to the internal Git platform

Data Stewardship & Security
- Handle sensitive data responsibly: redaction, access control, retention, auditability, and secrets management
- Document prompts, rules, and decision logic; improve observability, CI, and operational tooling
