# Authentic — New Grad Software Engineer
New York, New York, United States; on-site.

Authentic helps insurance brokers create custom insurance products by providing technology, compliance, and partnerships with insurance carriers. It builds the infrastructure for specialized insurance programs and is a Series A company.

## Responsibilities
- Build and ship features across our React frontend and Python/FastAPI backend
- Collaborate with product, design, and operations teams to solve real customer problems
- Contribute to internal tools that help our team work more efficiently
- Work with data systems that drive risk assessment and claims decisions
- Participate in code reviews, architecture discussions, and team rituals

## Requirements
- B.S. or M.S. in Computer Science or a related field (recently graduated)
- Experience building web applications (personal projects, coursework, or internships)
- Comfort working across the stack; curiosity about frontend and backend
- Strong fundamentals in data structures, algorithms, and software design
- Ability to work on-site in our New York office

## Nice to Haves
- React, Python, FastAPI, or PostgreSQL
- AWS or GCP
- Interest in fintech, insurtech, or regulated industries
- Prior internship experience in fast-paced environments

## Benefits
Competitive salary, equity, role trajectory, comprehensive family health benefits, 401(k) match, unlimited PTO, paid parental leave.
