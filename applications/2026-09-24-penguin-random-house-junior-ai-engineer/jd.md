## Job Description

Penguin Random House's Data Science team is hiring a Junior AI Engineer to help unlock the potential of AI and emerging technology through practical tools that improve how teams work. PRH uses AI and machine learning to shape book discovery, sales, and customer engagement, and leverages data science across marketing, pricing, forecasting, personalization, and more. Looking for strong Python skills, product sense, and sound judgment.

Responsibilities:

- Build tools, automations, and applications that support internal business needs across marketing, research, and workflow optimization.
- Design, test, and improve language model based workflows for tasks such as classification, summarization, extraction, generation, and search support.
- Partner with teams across Marketing, Product, Data Science, and the broader business to translate needs into clear, scalable solutions.
- Develop Python-based services, APIs, and internal tools that integrate AI capabilities into day-to-day workflows.
- Evaluate and refine system performance for quality, speed, reliability, and cost.
- Identify edge cases, improve system behavior, and contribute to best practices for applied AI development.
- Stay current on the rapidly evolving AI landscape and help evaluate which tools and approaches are worth adopting.

- Strong Python skills and a practical approach to building useful, reliable, and responsible solutions

Preferred:
