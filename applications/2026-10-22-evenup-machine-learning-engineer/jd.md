# EvenUp — Machine Learning Engineer

- Location: San Francisco (hybrid); Toronto (hybrid), 3 days/week in office
- Employment type: Full time
- Department: Data Science
- Compensation: San Francisco base $130K – $205K + equity; Toronto base CA$110K – CA$170K + equity

## About
EvenUp is on a mission to close the justice gap using technology and AI. It empowers personal injury lawyers and victims to get the justice they deserve, helping law firms secure faster settlements, higher payouts, and better outcomes for victims injured in vehicle collisions, accidents, natural disasters, and more. Backed by Bessemer Venture Partners, Bain Capital Ventures, SignalFire, and Lightspeed.

## Machine Learning Engineer
Join the ML team to develop and deploy models that power Piai™, EvenUp's proprietary claims-intelligence platform, with a focus on machine learning, natural-language processing, and generative AI. Work alongside senior ML engineers, data scientists, and legal subject-matter experts to turn raw legal and medical data into production-ready models.

## What You'll Do
- Explore, implement, and benchmark ML, NLP, and generative AI methods, including LLM fine-tuning, retrieval-augmented generation, and document understanding.
- Clean, annotate, and transform structured and unstructured case data, and build reusable datasets and data loaders.
- Design experiments, run A/B tests, analyze results, and communicate findings to product and engineering teams.
- Help integrate models into production systems, partnering with ML platform engineers on testing, monitoring, and scaling.
- Pair with product managers, legal analysts, and software engineers to translate real problems into ML solutions and measurable improvements.
- Stay current with research in LLMs, representation learning, and prompt engineering, and share what you learn with the team.

## What You Bring
- B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Data Science, Statistics, Computational Linguistics, or a related field.
- Solid grounding in ML fundamentals: supervised and unsupervised learning, evaluation metrics, overfitting and regularization.
- Hands-on experience with NLP or generative AI techniques: transformers, embeddings, sequence-to-sequence models, or LLMs.
- Proficiency in Python and ML/NLP libraries such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face, or spaCy.
- Familiarity with SQL and basic data engineering concepts: ETL, versioned datasets, notebooks.
- Strong analytical thinking, clear communication, and eagerness to learn and iterate quickly in a fast-moving startup.
- Ability to work hybrid (3 days your choice) in the San Francisco or Toronto office.

## Nice to Have
- Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, GCP).
- Experience with experiment-tracking tools (Weights & Biases, Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, MLflow).
- Familiarity with containerized deployment (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Experience in a high-growth startup environment.

Medical, dental, vision; life/accident/critical illness options; flexible PTO; 10 US holidays / Canadian statutory holidays; home office stipend; 401(k) / RRSP; paid parental leave; local meet-up program; hubs in San Francisco and Toronto.
