# Stripe — Software Engineer, New Grad

Team: University
Office locations: New York, Seattle, South San Francisco HQ
Employment type: Full time
US base salary: $122,100 – $134,400

## About Stripe

Stripe builds programmable financial infrastructure so any business can start and scale. More than 10 million businesses build on Stripe and process trillions of dollars a year. They maintain some of the most reliable APIs on the internet, ship product upgrades every day, and invest heavily in risk and fraud infrastructure.

They admire ambition, intensity, curiosity, humility, and rigor. Individual agency has outsized impact; small teams can change how millions of businesses grow.

## Who you are

- Ambitious builder: solutions without clear precedent; curious; prefer discovery to certainty
- Rigorous thinker: things worth doing are rarely simple; problems that have never been tackled
- Adaptable problem solver: treat obstacles as opportunities; measured risks; act without consensus

## Minimum requirements

- Bachelor’s or Master’s in CS or related field by summer 2027, or equivalent experience; new grads ≤18 months professional experience (excluding internships)
- Programming via side projects or classwork. Stripe works mostly in Java, Ruby, JavaScript, Scala, and Go
- Internships or collaborative multi-person coding projects
- Learn unfamiliar systems independently and with mentors
- Clear written communication: explain work to stakeholders and teammates
- Leverage AI tools to accelerate development while applying rigorous critical thinking to review, refine, and validate all outputs

## Preferred qualifications

- Specialized knowledge (frontend, backend, infrastructure, or other) plus general skills
- Code review practices and how to safely update production systems
- Navigating and managing work in large code bases
- Leading small projects independently and contributing to larger projects alongside peers
- High agency: unblock yourself, know when to ask for help

## In-office

At least 50% of the month in local office or with users (varies by team/location).
