## About Abridge

Abridge was founded in 2018 with the mission of powering deeper understanding in healthcare. Its AI-powered platform was purpose-built for medical conversations, improving clinical documentation efficiencies while enabling clinicians to focus on their patients.

Enterprise-grade technology transforms patient-clinician conversations into structured clinical notes in real time, with deep EMR integrations. Powered by Linked Evidence and purpose-built, auditable AI, Abridge maps AI-generated summaries to ground truth, helping providers quickly trust and verify the output.

Team of practicing MDs, AI scientists, PhDs, creatives, technologists, and engineers. Offices in San Francisco (Mission District), New York (SoHo), and Pittsburgh (East Liberty).

## The Role

Early-career Software Engineers who are highly driven and passionate about real-world impact through AI for healthcare. Join a cross-functional team of researchers, clinicians, and engineers to translate cutting-edge language model capabilities into dependable clinical systems. Build AI-powered features that touch real clinical workflows, ship production code, and grow fast in an environment where GenAI is at the center of everything.
