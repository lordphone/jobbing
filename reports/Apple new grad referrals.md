# Target Apple's hidden early-career reqs, then the team

Yes, Apple has open entry-level SWE postings right now, but none of them says "new grad." As of 2026-09-29, jobs.apple.com shows **two explicitly early-career SWE roles, both in IS&T in Austin**: **200677645** (Software Engineer, AiDP Reliability Engineering) and **200676168** (Frontend Engineer, EE&P). There are also **about 20 other software-type roles at ICT2**, Apple's entry level, or open to "0-2 years" of experience, spread across Cupertino, Sunnyvale, San Diego, Austin and Orlando. On referrals, **any Apple employee can technically submit you for any posted req**. Nothing in the evidence requires the referrer to be on the hiring team. In practice, though, Apple hires team by team, and Apple employees say again and again that a generic referral from an unrelated org is mostly ignored. What gets interviews is someone on or near the team, or someone who personally emails the hiring manager or recruiter with the req link. That changes how you should use LinkedIn. Pick a specific req number first. Then find alumni or engineers on that team and ask for information before you ask for a referral. Also send the hiring manager a short direct note, because at Apple the hiring manager effectively owns the req. One caveat applies throughout: Apple publishes nothing official about its referral program. Most insider evidence comes from Blind threads dated 2018-2024, and the 2025-2026 guides are marketing content with unsourced numbers.

## About 20 entry-level SWE reqs are live, and none is labeled "new grad"

Searches on Apple's US job board for "new grad," "university graduate," "recent graduate," "new graduate" and "graduating" all return **zero results**. Apple's Students page offers only internships and co-ops. It has no full-time program for recent grads ([Apple Students](https://www.apple.com/careers/us/students.html)). So new-grad hiring runs through ordinary reqs that each team posts at **ICT2**, and sometimes at ICT3 with a "0-2 years" requirement. The closest thing to a program is the **IS&T "Early Career" label**, whose postings mention an "Early Career community" with educational and social events ([200676168](https://jobs.apple.com/en-us/details/200676168/frontend-engineer-ee-p-is-t-early-career)). A third-party guide says the same thing: new grads join at ICT2, and reqs go up "on a rolling, team-by-team basis starting in the fall" ([Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/blog/apple-new-grad-software-engineer-2027)). That guide cites no sources.

## Anyone at Apple can refer you, but only the team's opinion counts

Apple's referral tool has no team restriction. An Apple employee on Blind offered referrals to strangers in unrelated orgs, asking only for a "req ID and resume." He said two of those candidates were hired and he received the referral bonus ([Blind, Jun 2022](https://www.teamblind.com/post/willing-to-do-apple-referrals-rqncfzgm)). Other Apple-verified employees have offered referrals for iOS new-grad, supply-chain and other roles outside their own teams ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/referral-for-apple-for-new-grad-ios-dev-roles-bnwoxoh5); [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/apple-new-grad-referral-pv5cfydv)). So the rule that the referrer must be on the team is **folk advice about effectiveness, not a submission requirement**.

The mechanics matter. Referrals are **tied to a specific req**, because Apple has no general SWE pool to refer someone into. The email the candidate receives says Apple will reach out "if you're a good match for the referred position" ([Blind, Jan 2024](https://www.teamblind.com/post/how-does-referral-at-apple-works-vpyppxra)). Several threads say **you must also apply to that same req yourself** on jobs.apple.com, or the referral won't link to your profile ([Blind, 2019](https://www.teamblind.com/post/how-does-apple-referral-works-ccmifu3a); [Blind, 2021](https://www.teamblind.com/post/question-about-apple-referral-process-ocybbmo2)). Order matters too. Search summaries of Blind threads say a referral may not count if you have already applied ([Blind, 2023](https://www.teamblind.com/post/Apple-referral-aLan6ek7)). So get the referral submitted first, then apply from the email or the portal. The system also breaks. One candidate twice failed to receive the referral email ([Blind, Apr 2024](https://www.teamblind.com/post/got-referral-for-apple-but-never-got-email-v16xjvug)). An Apple employee relayed a recruiter's 2021 comment that the "referral system was broken" and that employees should ping the recruiter directly ([Blind, Jun 2021](https://www.teamblind.com/post/how-important-is-referral-in-apple-eaoaqr3n)).

The tool accepts anyone, but whether the referral works depends on who sends it. Apple employees are blunt about generic submissions. "I've referred probably 50 people and I don't think any of them heard back," one wrote ([Blind, 2018](https://www.teamblind.com/post/How-do-Apple-referrals-work-nVW32zTS)). Another reported "0% success rate" and said referrals work "if the person referring knows HM and contacts them directly" ([Blind, 2022](https://www.teamblind.com/post/do-referrals-work-at-apple-neiyegwq)). A third said "referrals are only useful if coming from the team you're trying for," because "each team has its own recruiter" ([Blind, 2021](https://www.teamblind.com/post/how-important-is-referral-in-apple-eaoaqr3n)). There is a more generous reading: a referral puts your resume at the top of the pile and breaks ties between similar candidates ([Blind, 2020](https://www.teamblind.com/post/does-a-referral-at-apple-actually-help-wxxs3fro)). Put together, the ranking is clear. **The strongest route is a team member or the hiring manager advocating for you personally. Next comes a referral from the same or an adjacent team, then a generic referral that brings visibility at best, and last a cold application.** Apple's form also has no formal "strong referral" or "worked with this person" field. The weight comes entirely from the referrer's relationship to the team.

Sources disagree on whether Apple pays a referral bonus. Simplify says "no cash referral bonus" ([Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/blog/apple-new-grad-software-engineer-2027)), but an Apple employee reported the bonus in his paycheck ([Blind, 2022](https://www.teamblind.com/post/willing-to-do-apple-referrals-rqncfzgm)). The firsthand report is more credible, and no amount is verified. The practical point is that some Apple employees describe a norm, or even a Business Conduct concern, against referring people they don't know. One wrote, "Anyone giving a personal referral about someone they don't know shouldn't work at Apple" ([Blind, 2019](https://www.teamblind.com/post/referral-for-apple-lanwx04y)). Others disagree, and the evidence dates from 2018-2021. Still, expect cold contacts to be cautious.

## Win LinkedIn by starting from a req number

Apple hires team by team, so your outreach should start from **one specific posting**, not from "Apple." Pull the team or technology suffix and two or three distinctive nouns from the posting, such as "AiDP," "Darwin runtime," "Core OS" or "signoff infrastructure." Then search LinkedIn people for current Apple employees with those keywords, filtered to your school and to 2nd-degree connections. That last step is a practical trick, not a sourced method. Apple's secrecy also works in your favor here. Postings rarely say which project you would join, so a same-team engineer can offer exactly what the posting can't: the team name, the hiring manager's name and what the work actually is ([Rolewyn](https://rolewyn.com/companies/apple/referral)).

Contact people in this order. Start with anyone who already knows you. Next come alumni or shared-context engineers on the target team, ideally mid-level ICs with 2-5 years at Apple rather than overloaded senior staff ([Rolewyn](https://rolewyn.com/companies/apple/referral); [Exponent](https://www.tryexponent.com/blog/how-to-get-an-apple-referral/)). Third is the hiring manager. At Google or Meta, messaging the hiring manager is a long shot. At Apple it is a real route, because the hiring manager owns the req. A Blind commenter advised new-grad applicants to "contact manager personally, it's team based," especially hiring managers with "I'm hiring" in their LinkedIn headline, and warned that online-only applicants often get ignored ([Blind, 2020](https://www.teamblind.com/post/tips-for-getting-to-apple-new-grad-swe-0qapmjcy)). Recent new-grad hires are useful for information but weak as referrers. Cold messages to recruiters mostly go unanswered ([Happenstance](https://happenstance.ai/referral/apple)).

The message should fit LinkedIn's limits. **Connection notes are capped at 200 characters**, and free accounts get only a small monthly quota of personalized notes, reported as 3 to about 10 ([Dripify](https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/8490987-limited-personalized-connection-request-notes-for-free-linkedin-accounts)). So the note is only a hook, for example: "Hi [Name], fellow [School] grad. I'm applying to Apple's [role, #200677645] and saw you work on [area]. Open to 2 quick questions about the team?" Once they accept, send a message **under about 100-150 words**. Include the shared context, the req number and link, two concrete lines of fit from your real projects, a small ask and an explicit "no worries if not" ([Happenstance](https://happenstance.ai/referral/apple); [DataVidhya](https://datavidhya.com/learn/de-interview-prep/resume-and-getting-the-interview/referrals-bypass-everything/)). The small ask can be 15 minutes, the hiring manager's name, or whether they'd be open to referring you. Add a two-sentence project summary they can forward to the hiring manager ([Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/blog/apple-new-grad-software-engineer-2027)). Given Apple's caution about referring strangers, ask for information first and the referral second. The exception is people who already know you, who can take the direct ask. When someone agrees, ask for two things: the formal referral for that req, and a short email from them to the req's recruiter or hiring manager with your resume attached. That email is the step Blind threads keep saying the tool alone doesn't do ([Blind, 2018](https://www.teamblind.com/post/How-do-Apple-referrals-work-nVW32zTS)). Follow up once after about 5 business days, stop after two messages, and move to another contact ([Rolewyn](https://rolewyn.com/companies/apple/referral)).

The payoff numbers are weakly sourced but consistent in direction. Guides claim 1-3% reply rates for generic messages and 15-30% for messages tied to a specific shared signal ([Rolewyn](https://rolewyn.com/companies/apple/referral)). A better-grounded figure, from Ashby's 2025 Talent Trends data as reported by Happenstance, is that **40% of referred candidates reach an interview versus 3% of inbound applicants** ([Happenstance](https://happenstance.ai/how-to/ask-for-a-referral-for-a-job)). That figure covers many companies, not only Apple, and the original report was not checked.

## Conclusion

The two open questions have one answer. Anyone at Apple can refer you, and there is plenty to apply to, but neither the tool nor the posting count is the constraint. Hiring decisions sit with individual teams, so outreach should run per req, not per company. The Austin IS&T Early Career reqs are the obvious first targets because they are explicitly built for people like you. The San Diego 0-2-year systems roles and the Cupertino and Sunnyvale SDET roles make a sensible second wave, with referral asks aimed at people on those specific teams. If you are location-flexible, lean toward Austin. If you are set on Seattle or NYC, no Apple entry-level SWE req exists there today, so outreach in those cities is networking for later postings, not referrals for current ones.

Two risks remain. The insider evidence on how referrals work is mostly three to eight years old, and Apple can quietly fill or pull any of these reqs. Check that each posting is still live before you spend a personalized connection note on it. Treat Apple as one channel among several: even candidates who pass the interviews can end up without an offer if no team has headcount ([Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/blog/apple-new-grad-software-engineer-2027)).
